# Group 3 verification (padmakosha sarpashirsha mrigashirsha simhamukha kangula alapadma chatura)
| hasta | verdict | key issue |
|---|---|---|
| padmakosha | MINOR | thumb too straight/low; fix thumb cmc_flex 40 abd 20 rot 25 mcp 15 ip 20 |
| sarpashirsha | MINOR | hood too shallow; all four mcp 25 pip 30 dip 20; spread (-8,0,8,12) |
| mrigashirsha | WRONG | index/middle/ring must fold ~80 at MCP (mcp 80 pip 30 dip 15); little upright (mcp -5, abd -6) not splayed; thumb erect cmc_flex -20 abd 5 |
| simhamukha | CORRECT | optional narrower V spread (4,0,0,-8) |
| kangula | WRONG | practice = thumb+index+middle tip pinch, ring folded, little erect (MoG 'another book'); idx/mid 50/35/15, ring F, little straight abd -10, T_OPP, contacts thumb-index,thumb-middle, apart little-ring |
| alapadma | MINOR | fan too narrow, progressive bend weak; spread (20,6,-14,-30 or max), ring 30/20/10, little 50/25/10, thumb flex -10 abd 35 mcp 10 ip 10 |
| chatura | MINOR | thumb tip should touch base of RING finger (ring_prox), not middle; three fingers identical (30/10/5 or straight); little mcp -5 abd -12; thumb flex 60 abd 0 rot 25 mcp 30 ip 30 |
Sources: Mirror of Gesture 1917 text + plates XI-XIII, Wikimedia Commons Category:Asamyuta Hastas, natyasutraonline.com gallery, nysadancecom.wordpress.com, kathakclub.com (secondary).
General pattern: refs concentrate flexion at the MCP more than our intents.
